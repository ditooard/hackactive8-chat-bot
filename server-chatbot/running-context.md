# RUNNING KB (Compact)

## 1. RACE DISTANCES & TARGETS
5K: 30-40m(pemula)/<20m(mahir) | 10K: 60-75m/<45m | HM(21.1K): 2:00-2:30/<1:30 | FM(42.2K): 4:00-5:30/<3:00 | Ultra: >42K Trail/Semi/Trail Ultra

## 2. TRAINING METHODS
Easy: Z2 60-70%HR 30-60m | Long: 70-80%HR 60-180m | Tempo: 80-88%HR 20-60m | Interval: 90-95%HR 1:1 ratio | Fartlek: speed play | Hill: uphill repeats | Recovery: 60-65%HR 20-30m | Rule: 10%/wk max increase, rest = adaptation

## 3. SHOES

Hemat 2.7-4.2% energi.

## 4. NUTRITION
Daily: CHO 55-65%, Protein 1.2-1.6g/kg, Fat 20-30% | Pre: CHO complex 2-4hr | CarbLoad: 8-12g/kg 24-48hr before | During: <1hr none, 1-2.5hr 30-60g/hr, >2.5hr 60-90g/hr (gel/isotonik) | Gel strat: KM 8/16/24/30/36 | Post: CHO 1g/kg + P 20-40g (3:1 ratio) dlm 30-60m | Hydrate: by thirst, >2% loss = down, overhydrate=hyponatremia | Caffeine: 3-6mg/kg

## 5. RECOVERY & LIFESTYLE
Sleep 7-9h | Stretch: dynamic pre, static post 20-30s | Strength 2x/wk (squat/lunge/plank) | Cross: bike/swim | Mind: chunking, breathing, visualize finish

## 6. COMMON INJURIES
ShinSplints: tibia pain, overload/heelstrike -> gradual buildup | RunnerKnee: patella pain, glutes weakness -> strengthen glutes | PlantarFasciitis: heel pain AM -> calf stretch+insole | ITBS: lateral knee, weak gluteus -> foam roll+strength | AchillesTend: posterior pain, overuse -> eccentric heel drops | Prevention: 10% rule, shoes 500-800km, warmup 5-10m, cooldown 5-10m | RICE protocol

## 8. RACE DAY
MentalChunk: 0-7K warmup, 7-14K rhythm, 14-21K halfway, 21-28K fight, 28-35K danger, 35-42K reward | Pace: start 5-10s slower, don't chase others | Taper 2-3wk: volume -40-60%, keep intensity | Gear: broken-in shoes, bib+chip, 3-5 gels, hat, sunscreen, vaseline | Don't try new food/drink on race day
